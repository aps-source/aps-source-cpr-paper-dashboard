What was tested and dropped

  • Trailing stops (breakeven, previous-bar low, VWAP): lower profit on every variant, worst loss barely changed.
  • Narrow-CPR filter on SVR: cuts profit by ~70-75% - kept only on RVR, where it fixed the second-half decay.
  • R1/PDH target for NIFTY SVR: hit only 6-8% of the time - NIFTY keeps the CPR BC target.
  • RVR on CRUDE and the exact mirror of SVR: net negative.

Caveats: many variants were tested on one 2026 sample; no result reaches t = 2. Not yet checked out-of-sample (BANKNIFTY/SENSEX, NIFTY 2025).
